FAQ

Common Questions

How long does production take?+
Most orders ship within 3–5 business days. During peak seasons (holidays, back-to-school) allow an extra 1–2 days. We'll email you when your order ships with a tracking number.
How many lines of text can I fit?+
It depends on the tag size and shape. Small oval and heart tags fit 2–3 lines. Round, hex, and rectangle tags can hold 4–5 lines. The customizer above will warn you if your text is too long.
What if I make a typo on my order?+
We review every order before engraving. If something looks off we'll reach out to confirm. If a typo is on your end, contact us within 2 hours of placing your order and we can usually catch it before production starts.
Are the tags safe for all dogs?+
Yes. All our materials are pet-safe and free of harmful coatings. Aluminum tags are our lightest option, great for small dogs, puppies, and cats. Brass and steel are heavier but virtually indestructible.
Do you ship internationally?+
Currently we ship to the USA and Canada. International shipping is on our roadmap. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when we expand.
Can I order in bulk for a rescue or shelter?+
Absolutely — we love supporting rescues. Email us at TeamBravoK9@gmail.com with your order size and we'll set up a custom discount. Orders of 20+ tags get at least 25% off.
